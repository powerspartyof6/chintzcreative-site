Watercolors

Painted by hand, one at a time.

Houses, bouquets, venues and pattern studies on cotton paper. The same instinct chintz began with: a flower drawn rather than reproduced.

Commissioning one

Send photographs and rough dimensions. You will see a pencil sketch for approval before any paint goes down. Most pieces take three to four weeks; wedding work is scheduled around the date.

Sizes and paper

Small8 × 10 in

Medium11 × 14 in

Large16 × 20 in

Cold-press cotton rag, archival pigment. Framing available.

Beyond paper

Paintings are often the start of something else — a repeat printed for a room, a monogram scaled up onto a banner, a venue drawing carried through invitations and menus.
