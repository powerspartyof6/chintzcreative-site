    Sizes

    Starts at letter size, grows with standard frames

    Each size is one flat price for a set number of items. Up to two more items can be added at $5 each. After that, or once the items fill the paper, the piece moves up a size, which holds more items and fits the next standard frame.

    PaperMat windowFrameBase priceItems includedMost itemsFrame price

    Each item past the included count adds , up to two. The mat window is the part that shows; the mat covers about half an inch of paper on each side.

    What counts as an item

    One price per item, flourishes free

    Every photo, date or set of words on the paper is one item, whether it is a year or a mascot. Laurels, sparkles, small leaves and one lettered line such as "happy 40th birthday" are part of every piece and never counted.

    Framing

    Wood, gold or black, matted and ready to hang

    Every framed piece gets a white mat. Letter size paintings go in an 11 × 14 frame matted to 8 × 10, and each size up moves to the next standard frame. The frame price is the same for all three styles.

    Where the numbers come from

    Hobby Lobby prices are its everyday 40% off frame prices from hobbylobby.com in September 2026. The Raleigh and Knightdale stores should match, but in-store stock varies. Michaels runs coupons often; at full price its 11 × 14 matted frames cost more than the $25 charge, so buy there only on sale.

    What the margin covers

    The trip to the store, fitting the mat and painting, the occasional cracked glass, and a little profit. Aim for at least $5 on the small frame and about a third on the bigger ones, where breakage costs more.

    Before charging these

    Check the 16 × 20 and 22 × 28 prices in store. Gold and natural wood styles sometimes cost more than black at the same size; if one does, price the size from the most expensive of the three.

    Open questions before this goes live

    This page is not linked from the site yet. These are the decisions it still needs.

    Should the example piece fit on letter paper?

    The real 1986 · BEK piece has 9 items, counting the trees and drumsticks. Under these rules that is past the 7 item limit for letter size, so it would move up to 11 × 14 ($100). The example on this page leaves those two off. If small accents like trees should not count, the limit could stay at 7 and accents could be free, like the flourishes.

    Do dates and short words take a place?

    Right now they do, like any other item. The alternative is to treat a few words and a year as free lettering, like the flourishes.

    The watercolor page lists 5x7 and 8x10. Do those stay?

    This board starts at letter size (8.5 × 11). The custom watercolor page and its FAQ still offer 5x7, 8x10, 11x14 and 16x20. Pick one set of sizes and use it on both pages.

    Is sending ready for the live site?

    Yes, through FormSubmit, the same service as the commission form. FormSubmit activates each web address separately, so the first board sent from chintzcreative.com will ask Bailey to click an activation link. Send one test from the live page after publishing. Payment can stay a Stripe invoice sent by hand at first.
